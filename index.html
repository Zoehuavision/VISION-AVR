01 — Quality control

Every unit is tested before it ships.

Four checkpoints run on the production line, from component placement to full-load aging.

AOI

Automated optical inspection

Detects misaligned components, missing parts, insufficient solder and cold joints on every board, cutting defects and raising reliability.

THERMAL

High/low-temperature testing

Military-spec cycle: every unit runs 4 hours at 85°C and is cold-soak tested at −45°C before it leaves the factory.

PARALLEL

Parallel-operation testing

Every model rated for parallel operation is tested in a multi-unit rig to confirm stable, synchronized output in the field.

AGING

8-hour high-temperature aging

Run at the AVR's maximum rated power and sampling voltage under NI LabVIEW automated monitoring, logging current, output voltage and ambient temperature throughout.
